A Windows utility by junocity

A little clarity before your next sync.

Check your folders for naming and path problems. Understand what needs attention, review each change, and keep a way back.

SyncReady is being prepared for its first Microsoft Store release. This site provides product information, support, and privacy details. Downloads and purchases are not available here.

01 / Check

Start with a folder.

Scan names, paths, and local metadata for Windows and common OneDrive compatibility risks. Scanning does not change your files or read their contents.

02 / Review

Make the choice yours.

Read the explanation, inspect the proposed name, and choose the changes you want. Items that cannot be safely renamed stay manual.

03 / Recover

Keep a way back.

Each repair saves a local recovery record before changing anything. Undo checks for later changes and stops if restoring a name would conflict.

Your files stay on your PC.

SyncReady does not upload filenames, folder structures, documents, scan results, or recovery records. It does not ask for your cloud account credentials. Microsoft Store handles the optional Pro purchase and license checks.

A compatibility check identifies risks; it cannot promise that every application or cloud service will accept a file. Linked and cloud-managed locations may be skipped when they cannot be inspected safely.
