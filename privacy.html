Your privacy

Your folders are your business.

SyncReady checks local metadata on your PC. It does not send your files, filenames, folder structures, scan results, or recovery records to the publisher.

Privacy policy for SyncReady, published by junocity. Updated 4 October 2026.

What the app checks

SyncReady reads file and folder names, locations, sizes, timestamps, filesystem attributes, and file identity metadata to find compatibility issues and validate renames. The scanner does not read document contents, upload directory structures, or connect to your cloud storage accounts.

The app includes no advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, generative AI APIs, or third-party cloud-storage integrations. Its scanning and repair features do not require a SyncReady account or an application server connection.

What stays on your PC

  • Preferences: appearance, compatibility settings, recent folder locations, destination settings, and the number of evaluation repairs used.
  • Scan results: findings are held in memory during the session. A report is saved only when you choose to export one.
  • Recovery records: original and new paths, operation times, file identity, status, and metadata needed to check whether Undo can safely reverse a rename. These records can contain sensitive filenames and error details, but no file contents.

Preferences and recovery records are stored in the app's local data directory. The app shows the relevant local paths in Settings and Repair history. They are not automatically sent to junocity.

Recovery records persist between launches. Keep the entire recovery folder, including its authentication key, while you may need Undo. Deleting records or the key removes the associated ability to undo. Uninstalling or resetting a packaged app may remove its local data; save the entire recovery folder first if you need an archive. An archive does not guarantee that Undo will remain possible after files have changed.

Microsoft Store and Windows

The optional Pro upgrade uses Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the account, purchase, payment information, and license delivery. SyncReady receives product pricing and license status, not your payment-card details or cloud storage credentials.

Store installation, updates, licensing, and operating-system diagnostics may involve Microsoft's services. Those services are covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement and your Windows privacy settings.

Reports and information you share

A CSV report contains file and folder paths and findings from the scan. Exporting creates a local file at your chosen location. Copying a path puts it on the Windows clipboard, which may be synchronized by Windows if you have enabled clipboard sync.

Diagnostic reports include the report time, app and Windows versions, selected profile, Pro status, scan and finding counts, error codes, and repair times and statuses. They exclude file paths and error text that might contain paths by default. Choosing Include file and folder paths in this report adds those details. SyncReady does not automatically send the report.

If you choose to contact support, junocity can see the details you submit and use them to investigate or respond to your request. The support page links to public GitHub issues: your issue, GitHub username, and anything you attach can be seen by other people. Do not post private filenames, personal details, recovery records, authentication keys, or unreviewed logs. Public posts may remain visible until edited or removed and may have been copied by others.

This website and GitHub

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or application cookies. The website host may process connection information to deliver the pages. If you follow a GitHub support link, GitHub handles the account and website activity under its privacy statement. These website services are separate from the app's local scanning and repair work.

Questions and changes

For a general privacy question, use the SyncReady support issue tracker and keep the message free of personal or confidential information. No private support email is currently published here.

Changes to how the app handles information will be reflected in this policy and its update date. Review the policy supplied with the version of SyncReady you use.
